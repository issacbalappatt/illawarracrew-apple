FAQ

Common questions

Can you clean our office after hours or on weekends?+

Yes, subject to availability and site access. We agree a schedule around your operating hours and confirm it in your quote. Weekend and NSW public holiday rates are higher.

How soon can you start?+

Tell us your preferred start date when requesting a free walk-through. We confirm the scope, access and available dates before you commit; an online request does not confirm attendance.

Are your cleaners insured and police-checked?+

Yes, every team member is police-checked and we carry public liability insurance. Certificate of Currency sent direct to your building manager on request.

Can you handle strata buildings?+

Yes, common areas, lobbies, lifts and stairwells across small-to-mid strata blocks throughout the Illawarra.

Do you supply consumables?+

On request we supply hand soap, toilet paper, paper towel and bin liners at cost.

Do we have to sign a long contract?+

Recurring office cleaning is generally month-to-month with a four-week notice period. Your written quote and service agreement confirm the arrangement and any notice requirements.

Can you send a Certificate of Currency to our building manager?+

Yes. We issue our Certificate of Currency direct to your building or strata manager, or to your HR team.

What is commercial cleaning?+

Commercial cleaning is the regular or one-off cleaning of business premises, offices, medical centres, retail, strata, gyms, warehouses, schools and hospitality venues. It covers workstations, kitchens, bathrooms, floors, glass and high-touch points to a commercial standard, typically performed after hours by an insured, police-checked crew.

How much does office cleaning cost in Wollongong?+

Office cleaning in Wollongong is quoted in writing after a free on-site walk-through. The price depends on the floor area, the number of bathrooms and kitchens, how often you need us and whether cleans are done after hours.

How is commercial cleaning priced?+

We price commercial work after a free walk-through, based on site size, frequency, scope and any after-hours access. You get the scope and price in writing before anything starts.

What do we agree before the first clean?+

The written scope sets out areas, tasks, frequency, access and price. Tell us about any reporting or site-specific paperwork requirements at the walk-through so they can be agreed before work starts.

How often should an office be cleaned?+

Frequency depends on staff numbers, visitor traffic, shared facilities and your standards. We discuss daily, weekly, fortnightly or one-off options at the walk-through and agree which tasks belong on each visit.

Do you clean medical centres and dental practices?+

Yes. We agree the routine cleaning scope, suitable products, precautions and access with your practice. COI and SWMS are available on request. We are a commercial cleaner and do not claim medical-grade accreditation.

Do you clean schools, childcare and government sites?+

Yes. We agree the cleaning scope, suitable products, precautions and access requirements for each site, with after-hours scheduling around student, patient or staff hours. The team is police-checked; COI and SWMS are supplied on request.

Do you cover Shellharbour, Dapto and Kiama businesses?+

Yes, our commercial team services the full Illawarra: Wollongong CBD, Shellharbour, Dapto, Kiama, Fairy Meadow, Corrimal and surrounding business districts.

When do we pay?+

Your quote and service agreement confirm payment arrangements. Recurring services are payable after each clean unless otherwise arranged. We accept bank transfer, credit card and approved electronic payment methods under our terms.
