1. Introduction

Illawarra Cleaning Crew ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, use our cleaning services, or interact with us.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a quote, book a service or create an account, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, service address, selected services, preferred dates, property details and the notes you provide. We also handle service, invoicing and payment information needed to manage your cleaning.

For an NDIS cleaning enquiry, you may provide your plan management type and plan manager details. Please share only the information needed to arrange cleaning; medical reports, diagnoses and full payment-card details are not needed in a general enquiry form. If someone makes an enquiry on your behalf, they should have your authority to provide your details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, arrange and deliver services, contact you about your booking, manage accounts and invoices, investigate concerns and meet applicable legal obligations. If you choose not to provide the contact or property information needed for a request, we may be unable to quote or arrange the service.

Providing details for an enquiry or booking allows us to contact you about that request. It does not by itself give consent to unrelated marketing. You can ask us to stop marketing messages using the contact details below or an unsubscribe option in a message.

4. Website and Service Providers

Our staff and service providers may handle relevant information to respond to your request and provide cleaning. The website also uses these technology providers:

  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers the website and supports address-search requests.
  • Supabase provides website account authentication and stores enquiries, quote requests and bookings.
  • Resend provides email delivery, including website and account messages.
  • Google provides address lookup, website analytics and advertising measurement.

Relevant details may be handled by these providers for their service functions, and information may also be disclosed where required by law. Some providers operate globally, so handling may occur in Australia or overseas under their service arrangements. Contact us if you need more information about provider handling for your request.

5. Analytics and Browser Controls

Google Analytics and Google Ads help measure website visits and responses to our advertising. These tools may use cookies and other identifiers according to your browser and Google settings. Address fields may send the search you enter for suggestions as you type, whether or not you select a suggestion.

You can manage cookies and site storage through your browser settings. Browser restrictions may affect features such as account sign-in.

6. Photographs and Reviews

We may take before and after photographs for service quality checks. Our service terms require your express consent before those photographs are published or used for marketing. We may separately request permission to use feedback in marketing.

7. Access, Correction and Privacy Concerns

Contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, discuss deletion or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before releasing or changing information. How long information is needed depends on the service, account, legal recordkeeping and any unresolved dispute; a deletion request may be limited by those needs.

We will review your request or complaint and explain our response. Your rights under applicable privacy laws remain unaffected. Where the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner has jurisdiction, you may also contact it about a privacy complaint.
