Common questions

Quick answers to what most Wollongong customers ask before they book.

What if my agent raises a cleaning concern?+
We confirm your end of lease cleaning scope during inspection. Send the final inspection report or written feedback identifying the concern so we can review it against that agreed scope and discuss the next steps. Cleaning does not guarantee a rental-bond refund. Your rights and remedies under the Australian Consumer Law remain unchanged.
How much does house cleaning cost in Wollongong?+
Weekly weekday house cleaning is $60/hr ex GST · $66/hr incl. GST, with a two-hour minimum of $120 ex GST · $132 incl. GST. A one-off weekday clean is $65/hr ex GST · $71.50/hr incl. GST. Fortnightly visits receive a smaller discount; weekend and NSW public holiday rates differ. See our pricing page for the full rate table.
What areas do you service?+
We service all suburbs across Wollongong and the greater Illawarra region, including Fairy Meadow, Dapto, Shellharbour, Kiama, Thirroul, Bulli, Corrimal, Figtree, Unanderra, Port Kembla, Albion Park, and more.
Do you support NDIS participants?+
Yes. We support Self-Managed and Plan-Managed NDIS participants across the Illawarra. We are not an NDIA-registered provider and cannot bill Agency-Managed plans directly. The team confirms the agreed cleaning tasks, funding arrangements and applicable tax treatment in your quote and service agreement.
How do I book a clean?+
Send a booking or quote request online 24/7. Choose your service and preferred date, then the team confirms the scope, price and availability by call or text. A request does not reserve a date until it is confirmed. You can also call 0416 592 879.
Do you bring your own equipment and products?+
Yes, we bring cleaning equipment and products. Tell us about allergies, children, pets, delicate surfaces and product preferences when requesting a quote so we can confirm suitable products and precautions. Follow product instructions and keep children and pets away from products and wet work areas.
Can you clean after hours or on weekends?+
Yes. After-hours and weekend cleans are available on request across Wollongong and the Illawarra, and are a standard option for offices and commercial sites. Saturday, Sunday and NSW public holiday bookings are charged at our weekend/holiday day rate. Tell us your preferred date and we'll confirm by call or text.
How soon can you start?+
Same-week bookings are often available and we do try to fit urgent jobs in around our existing runs. Tell us the week you need in the booking or quote form and we'll confirm what we can do by call or text.
