Who We Are Today

A team dedicated to excellence

Today, Illawarra Cleaning Crew stands as a testament to what passion and dedication can achieve. Our team of professional cleaners shares the same values that started it all.

We've cleaned hundreds of homes, helped countless families get their bond back, and built relationships that go beyond just business.

Who we are

Illawarra Cleaning Crew is operated by Illawarra Crew Pty Ltd, ABN 19 685 930 143, a local Wollongong cleaning business. Our team is police-checked and covered by $20M public liability insurance on every booking. Some of our cleaners also hold a current Working With Children Check, and we can send one of them on request.

Our services

Core cleaning services include residential cleaning, bond cleaning Wollongong with an inspection-defined scope, deep cleaning, office cleaning, NDIS cleaning for self-managed and plan-managed participants, and builders cleaning.

We also offer outdoor and add-on services, all quoted on inspection except carpet cleaning, which is $66 per room including GST with a $132 minimum: carpet cleaning, window cleaning, solar panel cleaning, gutter cleaning, pressure washing, and lawn mowing and garden care.

Where we clean

We service the whole Illawarra region, from Helensburgh in the north to Kiama in the south, including Wollongong, Shellharbour and the suburbs in between.

How booking and quoting works

Get an instant estimate on our quote page, then confirm your clean through our online booking system, available 24/7. Outdoor and add-on services are quoted after a quick inspection so pricing reflects the actual size and condition of the job. End of lease cleaning scopes are confirmed at inspection.

Who operates Illawarra Cleaning Crew?+

Illawarra Cleaning Crew is operated by Illawarra Crew Pty Ltd, ABN 19 685 930 143, a locally based cleaning business serving the Illawarra region.

What areas do you service?+

We service the Illawarra region from Helensburgh to Kiama, including Wollongong and Shellharbour and the suburbs in between.

Is your team insured?+

Yes, we carry $20M public liability insurance on every job, and our cleaners are police-checked. If you need a cleaner with a current Working With Children Check, tell us when you book and we will confirm who is available.

What if my agent raises a cleaning concern?+

Send the final inspection report or written feedback so we can review the concern against the cleaning scope agreed at inspection and discuss next steps. Your rights and remedies under the Australian Consumer Law remain unchanged.

How much does carpet cleaning cost?+

Carpet cleaning is $66 per room including GST, with a $132 minimum per booking. Other outdoor and add-on services such as window, solar, gutter, pressure washing and lawn or garden work are quoted after a quick inspection.
