Bond refunds

How bond refunds work in NSW

In NSW your bond is held by NSW Fair Trading, not the agent. After the final inspection, you and your agent or landlord agree on the amount and lodge the claim through Rental Bonds Online.

If everyone agrees and the details are correct, the refund is usually paid within about two business days of the claim being accepted. If one side lodges a claim alone, the other side has 14 days to respond or dispute it. If nobody disputes it, Fair Trading pays the claim after 14 days.

Disputes that cannot be settled go to the NSW Civil and Administrative Tribunal (NCAT). The ingoing condition report is the reference point, so keep your copy and take dated photos when you move out.

We confirm the cleaning scope during the inspection. If your real estate agent raises a cleaning concern at the final inspection, send us the inspection report or written feedback so we can review it against the agreed work. Please report concerns promptly, preferably within 72 hours of inspection. A report made more than 7 days after the clean does not remove your statutory rights. Damage, wear and permanent marks are separate from ordinary cleaning. See the full scope and concerns process.

Source: NSW Government, Getting your bond back

Nothing at all, they are the same service. "Bond clean" and "end of lease clean" are just two names for the full property clean you do when moving out to get your rental bond back. Real estate agents and renters across Australia use both terms interchangeably, so whether you search for bond cleaning or end of lease cleaning in Wollongong, you are booking the same thing.
