FAQ

Choosing a service, common questions

How do I choose between residential, deep clean and end of lease cleaning?+

Choose residential cleaning for regular upkeep, deep cleaning for a first clean or an occasional reset, and end of lease cleaning when you need your bond back and a property manager checklist. If your home hasn't been professionally cleaned in a while, a deep clean first makes ongoing residential cleans easier to maintain.

Are your prices hourly or fixed?+

Regular house, deep and NDIS cleans are billed hourly per cleaner, with a minimum booking length. End of lease is a fixed-price quote after a free on-site inspection, or hourly if you prefer. Windows, solar, gutters, pressure washing, lawn and garden are quoted on inspection, and carpet cleaning is charged per room. See the Pricing page for exact rates.

Do you offer window, solar, gutter, pressure washing or lawn services on their own?+

Yes, each of these can be booked as a standalone service or added on to a house or end of lease clean. All are quoted after a free on-site inspection so the price reflects your property.

How is carpet cleaning priced?+

Carpet cleaning is charged per room at the rate shown on our Pricing page, with a minimum charge per booking, all including GST.

How do I book a service?+

Use the quote or booking form to send us your service type, address and preferred date, or call or text us. No payment is taken online, we confirm the details and lock in a time by phone or text.

Can I combine services in one visit?+

Yes, add-on services such as window or carpet cleaning can usually be scheduled alongside a residential, deep or end of lease clean. Mention this when you request your quote so we can plan the visit.
