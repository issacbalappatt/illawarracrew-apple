FAQ

Common questions

How much does lawn mowing cost in Wollongong?+

Lawn and garden work is quoted after a free inspection so we can price your actual yard size, slope and access. All prices include GST where applicable.

Do you offer regular visits?+

Yes. Weekly, fortnightly or monthly visits are available. Request schedule changes at least 24 hours in advance; rescheduling is subject to availability and the published terms. There are no lock-in contracts.

Do you take the green waste away?+

Yes. Clippings, prunings and weeds are removed and taken away, so you are not left with a pile on the nature strip.

Can NDIS participants use this service?+

Yes. We support Self-Managed and Plan-Managed NDIS participants. We are not NDIA registered, so we cannot invoice the NDIA directly for Agency-Managed plans.

Can you combine lawn mowing with house cleaning?+

Ask us to assess both services. A shared visit depends on access, the agreed scope and team availability. We confirm the work, timing and any combined price in your written quote.

Do you do one-off tidy-ups?+

Yes. One-off tidy-ups are popular before a sale, an inspection or a vacate, and you are under no obligation to book a regular visit afterwards.
