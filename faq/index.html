Find answers to common questions about our professional cleaning services in Wollongong and the Illawarra region.

End of Lease Cleaning

What is included in your end of lease cleaning service?
We confirm the cleaning scope during the inspection. It can cover kitchen surfaces and cupboards, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, laundry, floors, skirting, window tracks and wall spot cleaning. Inside-oven detail, carpet steam cleaning, interior glass and other add-ons must be agreed in the quote; inclusions depend on the option and property condition.
Do you guarantee I'll get my bond back?
No cleaning service can guarantee the return of your entire rental bond. We confirm the cleaning scope at inspection. Send the inspection report or written feedback if a cleaning concern is raised so we can review it against the agreed scope. Damage, wear and non-cleaning deductions are separate matters. Your non-excludable consumer rights remain unchanged.
How long does an end of lease clean take?
The time depends on the size, condition and agreed scope. Hourly end of lease cleaning has a six-hour minimum, billed per cleaner. This is billable cleaning time, so two cleaners working three hours contribute six cleaner-hours. We confirm the likely duration and any fixed-price scope after inspection.
Do I need to be present during the cleaning?
No, you don't need to be present. Just ensure we have access to the property and all utilities are connected. We'll send you photos when the cleaning is complete.
What happens if the property manager finds something you missed?
Contact us promptly with the inspection report or written feedback identifying the cleaning concern. We review the report against the scope agreed at inspection and discuss the next steps with you. Our reporting process does not limit your rights or remedies under the Australian Consumer Law.

Residential Cleaning

How often should I have my home professionally cleaned?
This depends on your lifestyle and needs. We offer weekly, fortnightly, monthly, or one-time cleaning services. Most families find fortnightly cleaning ideal for maintaining a clean home.
Do you bring your own cleaning supplies and equipment?
Yes, we bring all professional-grade cleaning supplies and equipment. Eco-friendly options are available on request. Tell us about allergies, pets and surface requirements so we can confirm suitable products and precautions.
Can I customize what areas you clean?
Absolutely! We can customize our service to focus on specific areas or skip certain rooms. Just let us know your preferences when booking.
Are your cleaners insured and background checked?
Yes, all our cleaners are fully insured, police checked, and professionally trained. We take the security and safety of your home seriously.

Office Cleaning

What commercial cleaning services do you offer?
We provide comprehensive office cleaning including workspace cleaning, bathroom sanitization, kitchen/break room cleaning, window cleaning, carpet cleaning, and specialized services like post-construction cleanup.
Can you clean outside of business hours?
Yes, we understand businesses need minimal disruption. We can schedule cleaning during evenings, early mornings, weekends, or any time that suits your business operations.
Do you provide cleaning supplies for offices?
Yes, we bring all necessary cleaning supplies and equipment. We can also work with your preferred products if you have specific requirements.
How do you ensure security in our office?

Can you provide regular ongoing cleaning contracts?
Absolutely! We offer flexible contracts for daily, weekly, or monthly office cleaning services with competitive rates for ongoing arrangements.

NDIS Cleaning

What is NDIS cleaning and who qualifies?
We provide household cleaning for Self-Managed and Plan-Managed NDIS participants when the tasks and funding arrangements are agreed. We are not an NDIA-registered provider and cannot bill Agency-Managed plans directly. Confirm that the support is covered with your plan manager or support coordinator.
How do I know if NDIS cleaning is covered in my plan?
Check your plan and ask your plan manager or support coordinator whether the household cleaning tasks are covered. We confirm the scope, invoicing and applicable tax treatment in the quote and written service agreement; qualifying supports may be GST-free when the plan, agreement and ATO requirements are met.
What cleaning tasks can be covered under NDIS?
NDIS cleaning can include general housekeeping, bathroom cleaning, kitchen cleaning, laundry assistance, and maintaining hygiene standards that support your health and wellbeing.
Are your cleaners trained to work with NDIS participants?
Every cleaner we send to NDIS participants is police-checked and briefed on respectful, discreet service. Tell us about access, communication and household needs before the visit. Where a participant's situation requires a Working With Children Check, we assign a cleaner who holds a current one.
How do I book NDIS cleaning services?
Contact us directly to discuss your NDIS plan and cleaning needs. We'll work with you to develop a cleaning schedule that fits your plan and goals.

Booking & Pricing

How do I get a quote for cleaning services?
Enter your cleaning and property details in our online quote form. Eligible hourly services show an estimate; we'll confirm your request and details. Commercial cleaning and fixed end of lease quotes require a free inspection. You can also call us for a quote.
What factors affect the cost of cleaning?
Pricing is based on property size (bedrooms/bathrooms), type of cleaning required, frequency of service, and any additional services like oven or carpet cleaning.
Do you charge extra for additional services?
Inside-oven detail, carpet steam cleaning, windows and other add-ons can change the price. Tell us what you need so the quote can confirm the inclusions and total before the clean. For hourly work, tasks are completed within the agreed scope and billable time.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept physical cash (notes and coins), bank transfer, card and payment links. Payment is due on completion before the team leaves, unless approved credit terms are arranged in advance. We provide an invoice and receipt for every payment method.
Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?
Yes, you can cancel or reschedule with at least 24 hours notice. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice may incur a cancellation fee.
Do you offer same-day cleaning services?
We try to accommodate same-day bookings when possible, especially for emergency cleaning. Contact us directly to check availability for urgent cleaning needs.
Can you clean after hours or on weekends?
Yes. After-hours and weekend cleans are available on request across Wollongong and the Illawarra, and are a standard option for offices, strata and commercial sites. Saturday, Sunday and NSW public holiday bookings are charged at our weekend/holiday day rate. Tell us your preferred date and we'll confirm by call or text.
How soon can you start?
Same-week bookings are often available and we do try to fit urgent jobs in around our existing runs. Tell us the week you need and we'll confirm what we can do.

Outdoor and Add-on Services

How much do window cleaning, solar panel cleaning, gutter cleaning, pressure washing, or lawn mowing and garden work cost?
These are all quote on inspection, priced after we see the property, its access and the amount of work involved, so there's no fixed rate to quote sight unseen. Carpet cleaning is the exception, at $66 per room incl. GST with a $132 minimum. Call, text or send photos and we'll give you a firm price before we book anything in.

What happens at the inspection for outdoor services?
At the inspection we walk the property, check access (ladders, roof pitch, garden size, hard surfaces to be pressure washed) and confirm exactly what's included before giving you a fixed quote. This applies to window cleaning, solar panel cleaning, gutter cleaning, pressure washing, and lawn mowing and garden work. Once you accept the quote we book in a time that suits you.
Can I combine window, gutter, solar, pressure washing or lawn work with a regular clean or end of lease clean?
Yes, these outdoor and add-on services can be combined with a regular clean or an end of lease clean and quoted together in the same visit where access allows. Let us know what you need at the time of booking so we can quote the full job and schedule the crew accordingly.
