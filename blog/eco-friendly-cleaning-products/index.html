Eco-Friendly Cleaning Products for Wollongong Homes

Quick answer: Eco-friendly cleaning options are available on request for homes across Wollongong and the Illawarra. Product suitability depends on the surface, task and people using the space. One-off weekday house cleaning is $71.50/hr including GST, with a two-hour minimum; weekly weekday visits are $66/hr including GST.


Choose products for the task and surface


A label such as natural, plant-based or eco-friendly does not make every product suitable for every home. Tell us about stone benchtops, timber finishes, delicate fabrics and manufacturer care instructions before your visit. We can confirm suitable cleaning options when we prepare your quote.


Discuss allergies, children and pets


Let us know about fragrance sensitivities, allergies and pets. We cannot promise that a product is risk-free or suitable for a medical condition. Product labels and current safety data sheets guide handling, ventilation, protective equipment and any required drying or re-entry time. Keep children and pets away from products and wet work areas.


Use products as directed


Follow the product label and surface care instructions. Do not mix cleaning products or assume a homemade mixture is a disinfectant. Store chemicals in their labelled containers and out of reach of children and pets. A general cleaning product and a disinfectant have different purposes; a disinfectant must be used according to its directions, including any contact time.


Make practical environmental choices


Ask about product options, dosing and packaging rather than relying on broad environmental claims. Avoid unnecessary product use and follow label directions for disposal. A biodegradable claim does not mean a product should be poured into stormwater drains or waterways.


Plan your house clean


Share your preferences when you request a quote. We will confirm the scope, suitable product options and total price before work is agreed. See our house cleaning service and GST-inclusive cleaning prices.


Frequently asked questions

Can I request eco-friendly cleaning options?

Yes. Tell us your preferences, surfaces and sensitivities when requesting a quote so we can confirm suitable options.

Are eco-friendly products always safe for children and pets?

No product is risk-free. Suitability and precautions depend on the product, label, safety data sheet, surface and household. Keep children and pets away from products and wet work areas.

Can homemade mixtures replace a disinfectant?

Do not assume a homemade mixture disinfects a surface. Use an appropriate product according to its label and do not mix cleaning products.
