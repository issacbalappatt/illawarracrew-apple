Professional Cleaning Secrets for Wollongong Homes



Living in Wollongong means dealing with unique challenges - from coastal salt air to the red dust that seems to settle everywhere. After cleaning hundreds of homes across the Illawarra, we've learned what works best in our local environment.



1. Combat Salt Air Damage


The coastal location means salt air affects everything from windows to outdoor furniture. Wipe down glass surfaces weekly with a vinegar solution (1:1 ratio with water) to prevent salt buildup that can cause permanent etching.



2. Red Dust Prevention


Wollongong's red soil gets tracked everywhere. Place quality door mats both inside and outside entrances, and vacuum them weekly. The investment in good mats saves hours of floor cleaning.



3. Tackle Humidity and Mould


Our humid climate creates perfect conditions for mould. Keep exhaust fans running for 30 minutes after showers, and clean bathroom ceilings monthly with a mould-prevention cleaner.



4. Window Cleaning Timing


Never clean windows in direct sunlight - the solution dries too quickly leaving streaks. Early morning or late afternoon works best in our climate.



5. Carpet Stain Emergency Response


For spills, blot (don't rub!) immediately with clean paper towels. Apply cold water from the outside of the stain inward to prevent spreading.



6. Kitchen Grease Prevention


Wipe down your rangehood filter monthly and replace every 3 months. This prevents grease from spreading throughout your kitchen and reduces cleaning time.



7. Bathroom Exhaust Fan Maintenance


Clean your exhaust fan cover every 3 months. A clogged fan can't remove moisture effectively, leading to mould and mildew problems.



8. Hard Water Solutions


Wollongong's water can be quite hard. Use white vinegar to remove limescale from taps and showerheads monthly to prevent permanent damage.



9. Vacuum Maintenance


Empty your vacuum when it's 2/3 full and clean or replace filters monthly. A well-maintained vacuum cleans more effectively and lasts longer.



10. Create a Cleaning Schedule


Daily: Make beds, wipe kitchen counters, load dishwasher
Weekly: Vacuum/mop floors, clean bathrooms, dust surfaces
Monthly: Deep clean appliances, wash windows, organize closets



These tips will help maintain your home between professional cleans, but remember - even the most diligent homeowner benefits from regular professional deep cleaning to tackle the jobs that are difficult or time-consuming to do yourself.

Frequently asked questions

How do I stop salt air from damaging my windows in Wollongong?

Wipe glass surfaces weekly with a 1:1 vinegar and water solution to prevent the salt buildup from coastal air that can cause permanent etching over time.

Why does my Wollongong home get so much red dust?

The Illawarra's red soil is easily tracked indoors on shoes and pets. Placing quality door mats at every entrance and vacuuming them weekly significantly reduces how much dust reaches your floors.

How can I prevent mould in a humid Illawarra bathroom?

Run the exhaust fan for at least 30 minutes after every shower and clean bathroom ceilings monthly with a mould-prevention product, since humidity is the main driver of mould growth in coastal homes.

What is the best time of day to clean windows in Wollongong?

Early morning or late afternoon is best, since direct sunlight dries cleaning solution too quickly and leaves streaks on the glass.

How often should I clean my rangehood filter?

Wipe the rangehood filter monthly and replace it every three months to stop grease building up and spreading through the kitchen, which also reduces how long a deep clean later takes.
