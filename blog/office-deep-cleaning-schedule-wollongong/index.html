Creating the Perfect Office Cleaning Schedule



A clean office isn't just about appearances - it directly impacts employee health, productivity, and your business's professional image. Based on our experience cleaning offices across Wollongong's CBD and surrounding business districts, here's how to determine the right cleaning frequency for your workspace.



Daily Cleaning Tasks



  • Empty waste bins and replace liners

  • Wipe down high-touch surfaces (door handles, light switches, keyboards)

  • Clean and sanitize toilets and washroom facilities

  • Vacuum high-traffic carpeted areas

  • Clean kitchen/break room surfaces and appliances

  • Spot clean windows and glass doors



Weekly Deep Cleaning



  • Comprehensive vacuuming of all carpeted areas

  • Mop hard floors throughout the office

  • Dust all surfaces including desks, shelves, and equipment

  • Clean windows inside and out

  • Sanitize shared equipment (printers, coffee machines, telephones)

  • Deep clean washrooms including tile scrubbing



Monthly Professional Services



  • Carpet steam cleaning and stain treatment

  • Deep clean air conditioning vents and filters

  • Comprehensive kitchen deep clean

  • Window track and frame cleaning

  • Light fixture and ceiling fan cleaning

  • Detailed cleaning of reception and common areas



Factors That Increase Cleaning Needs



High Foot Traffic


Reception areas, corridors, and meeting rooms near entrances need daily attention. The red dust common in Wollongong gets tracked in constantly.



Food Service Areas


Office kitchens and break rooms require daily cleaning to prevent pest problems and maintain hygiene standards.



Open Plan Offices


Shared spaces spread germs quickly. Increase surface sanitizing during flu season (May-September in our climate).



Client-Facing Businesses


If clients visit regularly, maintain higher standards with more frequent deep cleaning and attention to presentation areas.



Industry-Specific Recommendations



Medical/Dental Offices


Daily deep sanitizing with hospital-grade disinfectants, weekly comprehensive cleaning.



Legal/Financial Services


Professional appearance is crucial - twice weekly cleaning minimum, with daily maintenance.



Creative/Tech Spaces


More relaxed but still professional - weekly deep cleaning with daily maintenance tasks.



Food Service Businesses


Daily deep cleaning required for health compliance, with specialized equipment cleaning.



Signs You Need More Frequent Cleaning



  • Employees frequently getting sick

  • Visible dust on surfaces

  • Unpleasant odors

  • Stained carpets or upholstery

  • Client complaints about cleanliness

  • Pest problems



Cost vs. Benefit Analysis


While more frequent cleaning costs more upfront, consider:



  • Reduced sick days and higher productivity

  • Better client impressions and business opportunities

  • Extended life of carpets, furniture, and equipment

  • Lower long-term maintenance costs

  • Improved employee morale and retention



Seasonal Considerations for Wollongong Offices



Summer (Dec-Feb)


Increase cooling system maintenance and window cleaning due to higher usage and coastal conditions.



Autumn (Mar-May)


Prepare for flu season with enhanced sanitizing protocols.



Winter (Jun-Aug)


Focus on moisture control and mould prevention due to higher humidity and closed windows.



Spring (Sep-Nov)


Deep clean to remove dust and allergens, perfect time for carpet cleaning.



Remember, every office is unique. A professional cleaning assessment can help determine the optimal schedule for your specific workplace, taking into account your industry, employee count, client expectations, and budget constraints.

Frequently asked questions

How often should an office be deep cleaned?

Most Wollongong offices need a full deep clean monthly, alongside daily tasks such as bin removal and high-touch surface wiping, and weekly tasks such as vacuuming, mopping and window cleaning.

How often should medical or dental offices be cleaned?

Medical and dental offices generally need daily deep sanitising with hospital-grade disinfectants plus a weekly comprehensive clean, given the higher hygiene standards these workplaces must meet.

Does carpet in an office need professional cleaning?

Yes, office carpets should be professionally steam cleaned roughly monthly as part of scheduled deep cleaning, since foot traffic and dust build up faster in commercial spaces than in homes.

What signs indicate an office needs more frequent cleaning?

Frequent staff illness, visible dust on surfaces, unpleasant odours, stained carpets or upholstery, client complaints and pest problems are all signs the current cleaning frequency is not enough.

Does office cleaning frequency change with the seasons in Wollongong?

Yes, winter calls for extra attention to moisture control and mould prevention due to humidity and closed windows, while spring is a good time for a deep clean to remove built-up dust and allergens.
