End of Lease Cleaning: Should You Do It Yourself?


Moving out of a rental in Wollongong is stressful enough without the looming threat of losing your bond. When it comes to the final exit clean, you have two choices: roll up your sleeves and do it yourself (DIY), or hire a professional end of lease cleaner. Which is the better choice for you?



The DIY Approach: Costs and Time


The main appeal of DIY cleaning is saving money. However, it's rarely "free". You need to factor in the hidden costs:



  • Cleaning Supplies ($50 - $100): You'll need heavy-duty degreasers, sugar soap for walls, mould removers, and quality sponges/cloths.

  • Equipment Hire: Carpet cleaning may require equipment or a separate quote. NSW tenancy agreements cannot generally require professional carpet cleaning; a permitted condition may apply where animals were kept indoors. Check your agreement and the carpet's condition rather than assuming every tenancy needs a professional receipt. See NSW Fair Trading's tenancy agreement guidance.

  • Your Time: NSW tenants must leave the property reasonably clean and in the same condition as at the start, allowing for fair wear and tear. Compare the entry condition report with the property now. Scrubbing an oven, detailing window tracks and cleaning agreed surfaces can take considerably longer than a regular maintenance clean.

The Verdict


If you are on a very tight budget, have 2-3 full days to spare, and don't mind physical labor, DIY is an option. Just make sure you follow a strict checklist.

Frequently asked questions

Is it cheaper to clean your own rental at the end of a lease?

DIY is usually cheaper in dollar terms, but only if you already own or hire the right equipment. Once you factor in cleaning supplies ($50 to $100), possible carpet cleaner hire, and 10 to 20 hours of your own time, the gap to a professional quote narrows considerably.

What happens if a DIY bond clean fails the final inspection?

If the agent isn't satisfied, they can arrange their own cleaner and deduct the cost from your bond, typically $150 to $400, and you have no guarantee of the standard or price of that re-clean.

How long does a DIY move out clean take?

Most renters need 10 to 20 hours to properly clean an average home to bond standard, covering the oven, bathrooms, windows, tracks and walls in the detail property managers expect.

Do professional end of lease cleaners guarantee the bond back?

Ask professional cleaners to confirm the inspection-defined scope and how they assess concerns. Cleaning does not guarantee a rental-bond refund, and your non-excludable consumer rights remain unchanged.

Is carpet cleaning cheaper to DIY or hire a professional?

Hiring a professional carpet cleaner is usually similar in cost to hiring a machine yourself once you include hire fees and cleaning solution, and it avoids the risk of over-wetting carpets or leaving marks that could cost you at inspection.
