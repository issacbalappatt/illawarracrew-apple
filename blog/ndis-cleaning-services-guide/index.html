NDIS Cleaning Services: Your Complete Guide



The National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) recognizes that a clean, safe living environment is essential for wellbeing and independence. If you're an NDIS participant in Wollongong or the Illawarra region, you may be eligible for funded cleaning services as part of your plan.



What Are NDIS Cleaning Services?


NDIS cleaning services fall under "Core Supports" in your plan, specifically under the category of "Assistance with Daily Living." These services help participants maintain a clean, hygienic, and safe home environment when they're unable to perform these tasks independently due to their disability.



Who Is Eligible?


You may be eligible for NDIS cleaning services if:



  • You have an approved NDIS plan

  • Your disability significantly impacts your ability to clean your home

  • The support helps you live more independently

  • Cleaning services are included in your Core Supports budget

  • The service is deemed "reasonable and necessary"



Types of Cleaning Services Covered



Regular Household Cleaning



  • Vacuuming and mopping floors

  • Dusting surfaces and furniture

  • Cleaning bathrooms and toilets

  • Kitchen cleaning and dish washing

  • Making beds and tidying rooms

  • Emptying bins and basic waste management



Deep Cleaning Services



  • Detailed bathroom sanitization

  • Kitchen appliance cleaning

  • Window cleaning (internal)

  • Carpet and upholstery cleaning

  • Seasonal deep cleaning tasks



Specialized Cleaning



  • Infection control cleaning

  • Cleaning around medical equipment

  • Allergen reduction cleaning

  • Cleaning adapted for specific disabilities



What's NOT Covered


NDIS cleaning services focus on essential household cleaning. These services typically don't include:



  • Garden maintenance or outdoor cleaning

  • Home repairs or maintenance

  • Cleaning that would normally be done by family members

  • Cleaning services for other household members

  • Pest control services

  • Window cleaning (external)



How to Access NDIS Cleaning Services



1. Check Your Plan


Review your NDIS plan to see if cleaning services are included in your Core Supports budget. Look for categories like "Assistance with Daily Living."



2. Choose Your Management Style



  • Agency Managed: NDIA manages payments directly to registered providers

  • Plan Managed: A plan manager handles payments and can work with both registered and unregistered providers

  • Self Managed: You manage all payments and can choose any provider



3. Find a Provider


Look for cleaning services that are:



  • Registered NDIS providers (required for Agency-Managed plans; not required for Plan-Managed or Self-Managed plans)

  • Experienced with disability support

  • Police checked and insured staff

  • Flexible and understanding of individual needs

  • Located in your area (Wollongong/Illawarra)



Benefits of Professional NDIS Cleaning



Health and Safety



  • Reduces risk of infections and allergies

  • Prevents accidents from clutter or spills

  • Maintains hygienic conditions for medical equipment

  • Creates a healthier living environment



Independence and Dignity



  • Helps maintain independent living

  • Reduces reliance on family and friends

  • Allows focus on other important activities

  • Preserves energy for social and recreational pursuits



Mental Wellbeing



  • Reduces stress and anxiety about home maintenance

  • Creates a comfortable, welcoming environment

  • Improves self-esteem and confidence

  • Enables social visits and activities at home



Working with Your Cleaning Provider



Initial Assessment


A good NDIS cleaning provider will conduct an initial assessment to understand:



  • Your specific cleaning needs

  • Areas of priority in your home

  • Any specific requirements related to your disability

  • Preferred cleaning products (especially for allergies)

  • Scheduling that works with your routine



Creating a Cleaning Plan


Your provider should develop a personalized cleaning plan that:



  • Addresses your specific needs and priorities

  • Fits within your NDIS budget

  • Is flexible enough to adapt as needs change

  • Includes clear communication protocols



Questions to Ask Potential Providers



  • Which plan types do you support? (registration is only required for Agency-Managed plans)

  • Do your staff have disability awareness training?

  • Are all staff police checked and insured?

  • Can you accommodate specific needs related to my disability?

  • What cleaning products do you use? (important for allergies/sensitivities)

  • How flexible are your scheduling options?

  • What happens if regular staff are unavailable?

  • How do you handle feedback and complaints?



Making the Most of Your Service



Communication



  • Be clear about your priorities and preferences

  • Provide feedback regularly

  • Discuss any changes in your needs

  • Keep communication channels open



Budget Management



  • Monitor your Core Supports budget regularly

  • Discuss frequency and scope with your provider

  • Plan for any seasonal variations in needs

  • Keep records for plan reviews



Plan Reviews and Changes


Your cleaning needs may change over time. During NDIS plan reviews:



  • Document how cleaning services have helped your independence

  • Note any changes in your disability or living situation

  • Provide evidence of how the service improves your quality of life

  • Discuss any adjustments needed to frequency or scope



NDIS cleaning services can significantly improve quality of life for participants by creating a clean, safe, and comfortable living environment. If you think you might be eligible, speak with your NDIS planner or plan manager about including these services in your next plan review.

Frequently asked questions

What NDIS funding category covers cleaning services?

Cleaning services are generally funded under Core Supports, specifically the Assistance with Daily Living category, provided the support is reasonable and necessary because your disability limits your ability to clean your home.

Does the NDIS require a registered provider for cleaning?

Only Agency-Managed plans require a registered NDIS provider. Self-Managed and Plan-Managed participants can choose any provider, registered or not, giving them far more flexibility in who they hire.

What cleaning tasks are not covered by the NDIS?

The NDIS typically does not fund garden maintenance, home repairs, cleaning for other household members, cleaning that would normally be done by family, pest control, or external window cleaning.

Can NDIS cleaning include carpet or upholstery cleaning?

Yes, deep cleaning tasks such as carpet and upholstery cleaning can be included where they are reasonable and necessary for a hygienic, safe living environment and fit within your Core Supports budget.

How do I start using NDIS funding for cleaning in Wollongong?

Check your plan for Assistance with Daily Living or Core Supports funding, decide whether you are Self-Managed or Plan-Managed, then contact a local provider directly to arrange an assessment and ongoing cleaning schedule.
