Getting Your Full Bond Back: The Complete Guide



Moving out of your rental property in Wollongong? Don't let poor cleaning cost you your bond! Property managers and real estate agents in the Illawarra region have specific standards, and knowing exactly what they're looking for can save you hundreds or even thousands of dollars.



Our professional cleaning team has worked with over 500 rental properties across Wollongong, Port Kembla, Fairy Meadow, and surrounding suburbs. We've seen what passes inspection and what doesn't. This comprehensive checklist is based on real feedback from local property managers.



🏠 Living Areas & Bedrooms



  • Walls: Remove all hooks, nails, and blu-tack. Fill holes with matching paint or putty

  • Skirting boards: Wipe down thoroughly - dust accumulates heavily here

  • Light fittings: Remove dust and dead insects from shades and globes

  • Ceiling fans: Clean all blades (top and bottom) and motor housing

  • Windows: Inside and outside glass, tracks, and frames

  • Window coverings: Dust blinds, wash curtains, or vacuum drapes

  • Carpets: Professional steam cleaning (keep receipts for property manager)

  • Hard floors: Mop thoroughly with appropriate cleaner for surface type

  • Air conditioning: Replace or clean filters, wipe down vents



🍳 Kitchen Deep Clean


The kitchen is often where bond claims happen. Wollongong property managers are particularly strict about:



  • Oven interior: Remove all grease, food residue, and burnt-on stains

  • Oven racks & trays: Soak overnight and scrub clean

  • Rangehood: Degrease filters and exterior surfaces

  • Stovetop: Clean burners, drip trays, and control knobs

  • Refrigerator: Empty, defrost, and clean inside and out

  • Dishwasher: Run cleaning cycle and wipe down seals

  • Cabinets & drawers: Empty completely and wipe inside and out

  • Benchtops: Use appropriate cleaner for stone, laminate, or timber

  • Splashback: Remove grease and food stains from tiles/glass

  • Sink: Remove limescale and food stains



🚿 Bathroom Sanitization



  • Shower/bath: Remove soap scum, mould, and limescale from all surfaces

  • Shower screen: Clean both sides, including tracks and runners

  • Toilet: Deep clean inside bowl, behind base, and all external surfaces

  • Vanity: Clean mirror, basin, taps, and storage areas

  • Exhaust fan: Remove dust and grime from cover and blades

  • Tiles & grout: Scrub grout lines and re-seal if necessary

  • Floor: Mop thoroughly, paying attention to corners and behind toilet



🏡 External Areas



  • Balcony/patio: Sweep and wash down surfaces

  • Garage: Remove all belongings and sweep clean

  • Garden beds: Remove weeds and return to original condition

  • Lawn: Mow and edge if required by lease agreement

  • External windows: Clean outside surfaces if accessible



📋 Final Inspection Tips


Document everything: Take photos before and after cleaning to protect yourself.


Check lease requirements: Some Wollongong rentals have specific clauses about professional cleaning.


Keep receipts: Property managers often require proof of professional carpet cleaning.


Schedule wisely: Book your cleaning 2-3 days before your final inspection to allow time for touch-ups.

🎯 Common Bond Claim Reasons in Wollongong


Based on our experience with local property managers, these are the most common reasons for bond deductions:



  1. Inadequate oven cleaning (especially burnt-on grease)

  2. Mould in bathroom/shower areas

  3. Carpet stains not professionally treated

  4. Kitchen rangehood and filters not properly degreased

  5. Windows and tracks not thoroughly cleaned



Don't let these costly mistakes affect your bond. Whether you tackle the cleaning yourself using our checklist or book our professional service, the key is thoroughness and attention to detail.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most common reason Wollongong tenants lose their bond?

Inadequate oven cleaning is the most common reason, followed by mould in bathrooms, carpet stains that were not professionally treated, and rangehood filters that were not degreased.

Do I need to patch nail holes before moving out?

Yes, most Wollongong leases require you to remove hooks, nails and blu-tack and fill any resulting holes with matching paint or putty, beyond fair wear and tear.

Do I need a receipt for carpet cleaning to get my bond back?

Yes, most property managers in the Illawarra require a receipt as proof that carpets were professionally steam cleaned, particularly if the lease specifies this as a condition of the tenancy.

When should I book my end of lease clean before the final inspection?

Book your clean two to three days before the final inspection where possible, so there is time to address any touch-ups without risking a missed handover deadline.

Is lawn mowing part of an end of lease clean?

Only if your lease requires you to return the garden to its original condition. Mowing and edging are external tasks that some Wollongong leases specify separately from the internal clean.
