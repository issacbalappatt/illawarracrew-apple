FAQ

Common questions

What's different about a deep clean vs regular clean?+

A deep clean makes time for detailed tasks such as inside the oven and fridge, grout, skirting, wardrobes and window tracks. Choose your priorities before the clean. What can be completed depends on the booked hours, condition and safe access.

How long does it take?+

As a guide, a two-bedroom home may need five to seven cleaner-hours and a three-bedroom home seven to ten. Larger or dirtier homes take longer. Hourly rates are per cleaner; the crew size affects the time spent at your property. Your quote confirms the estimated hours and total.

How much does deep cleaning cost in Wollongong?+

Deep cleans start from $260 ex GST · $286 incl. GST, the four-hour weekday minimum. The weekday hourly rate is $65/hr ex GST · $71.50/hr incl. GST per cleaner. A three-bedroom guide is $450–$650 ex GST · $495–$715 incl. GST, depending on condition and scope. Your quote confirms the hours, crew and total; weekend and public holiday rates are higher.

Do I need to be home?+

You do not need to stay for the whole clean. Agree key or lockbox access with us beforehand. We ask you to be available for a final walkthrough; if that is not possible, discuss the handover arrangements when booking.

Do you cover Shellharbour, Kiama and Dapto?+

Yes. We cover Wollongong and the Illawarra from Helensburgh to Kiama, including Shellharbour and Dapto. Tell us your address and preferred date so we can confirm availability.

Can you work around product sensitivities?+

Tell us about sensitivities and product preferences before booking. We discuss suitable products and precautions for your surfaces; we cannot promise an allergy-free result. Customer-supplied products may be used where safe and appropriate under our terms.

Will everything on the checklist be done?+

The checklist is a menu of tasks, rather than a promise to finish every item in a limited booking. Send us your priorities so we start with what matters most. We review the work with you at the end and discuss anything that needs more time.

Is oven cleaning included?+

Inside-oven cleaning can be a priority in your deep-clean checklist, along with the rangehood and kitchen. Tell us about its condition when requesting your quote so we allow suitable time. Permanent marks or damaged surfaces may not be removable.

Can I book a move-in or spring clean?+

Yes. A deep clean can prepare an empty home before moving in, or refresh an occupied home for spring. Tell us which rooms and tasks matter most; the quote confirms the hours and scope.

Do you bring products and equipment?+

Yes, we bring cleaning products and equipment. Let us know about any product preferences, delicate surfaces or access restrictions when requesting your quote.

What if I need to cancel or change the date?+

Please give at least 24 hours notice. Late cancellations may incur a fee under our terms. A new date is subject to availability, so call or text us to arrange it.

When do I pay?+

Payment arrangements are confirmed at booking under our terms. Bank transfer, credit card and approved electronic payment methods are accepted; a quote request does not take payment.
