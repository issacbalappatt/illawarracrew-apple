FAQ

Common questions

How much does window cleaning cost?+

Window cleaning is quoted after a free on-site inspection so we can give you a firm, fixed price for your exact glass area, height and access. All prices include GST where applicable.

How often should I have my windows cleaned?+

Most Illawarra homes benefit from a clean every 3-6 months. Coastal properties in Thirroul, Austinmer, Wollongong and Kiama often need more frequent cleans due to salt spray build-up on the glass and frames.

Do you clean high or hard-to-reach windows?+

Yes. Our team is equipped with extension poles, ladders and water-fed systems for most two-storey homes. For very high or unsafe access we will let you know up front during the quote.

Are frames, tracks and fly screens included?+

Yes. Every window clean includes wiping frames and surrounds, vacuuming and wiping tracks and sills, and reinstating fly screens after cleaning.

Can I book windows with my regular clean?+

Absolutely. Many customers add a window clean to their regular fortnightly or monthly house clean. Just mention it in the quote form and we will package it for you.

How do you achieve a clear finish?+

We use professional squeegees, microfibre cloths and suitable glass-cleaning solutions, then check the glass for streaks. If you have a concern after the clean, contact us so we can discuss it under our service terms.

How long does a window clean take?+

Timing depends on the number of windows, their condition, height and access. We assess these at the free inspection and confirm the scope, timing and fixed price before you book.

Which areas do you cover?+

Wollongong and the Illawarra, from Helensburgh to Kiama. Tell us your address and preferred date so we can confirm availability.

Can I request particular cleaning products?+

Tell us about product preferences and delicate surfaces before the quote. We agree suitable methods and precautions; customer-supplied products are used only where safe and appropriate under our terms.
