FAQ

Common questions

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?+

Yes, we bring all necessary cleaning supplies and equipment. If you prefer us to use specific products, just let us know.

How long does a standard clean take?+

A standard clean typically takes 3-4 hours for a medium sized home, but the duration depends on the size of your property and its condition.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?+

No, you do not need to be present during the cleaning. Many customers provide a key or access instructions for our team.

Are your cleaning products pet-friendly?+

Tell us about children, pets, allergies and product preferences before the visit so we can confirm suitable products and precautions. Follow product instructions and keep children and pets away from products and wet work areas.

What happens after I send an enquiry or quote request?+

Our team reviews your details and aims to reply within 2 business hours. After-hours enquiries are handled the next business morning. We confirm the scope, price and availability with you; an online request does not reserve a date.

What is your cancellation policy?+

We require 24 hours notice for any cancellation or rescheduling to avoid a cancellation fee.
