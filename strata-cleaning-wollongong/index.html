FAQ

Common questions

How is strata cleaning quoted in Wollongong?+

After a free walk-through of the building. We note the number of levels, lift access, the bin room, the scope of work and how often you need us, then provide a written scope and per-visit price you can table at committee.

What does strata cleaning include?+

Strata cleaning covers the shared areas of a building: entry and lobby, lifts, stairwells and landings, corridors, mailbox area, glass and door frames, bin rooms and bin bay wash-downs, carpark sweeping, and high-touch points such as handrails, intercoms and door handles. Scope is agreed in writing with the strata manager or committee before we start.

How much does strata cleaning cost in Wollongong?+

Strata cleaning is quoted after a free on-site walk-through, based on the size of the building, how often you need us and the scope of work. Levels, lift access and bin room condition all affect the price, so we put the scope and cost in writing rather than quoting a rate over the phone.

How often should common areas be cleaned?+

Most small-to-mid blocks book weekly or fortnightly, with bin rooms serviced weekly and carpark sweeps, high dusting and glass on a monthly or quarterly rotation. Buildings with heavy foot traffic or short-stay tenants usually need weekly.

Do you work with strata managers or the owners corporation?+

Both. We can invoice the strata manager directly or deal with the committee, whichever your building prefers. Scope, frequency and pricing are set out in writing so it can be tabled at a committee meeting.

Do you send a Certificate of Currency to the strata manager?+

Yes. We carry public liability insurance and every cleaner is police-checked. We send our Certificate of Currency directly to your strata manager or building manager on request.

Is there a lock-in contract?+

No lock-in. Most strata clients run month-to-month with a 4-week notice period, so the committee can review the arrangement at any time.

Do you clean bin rooms and do bin presentation?+

Yes. We clean and deodorise bin rooms, hose down bin bays where a tap and drain are available, and can add bin presentation and return to the schedule for kerbside collection days.

Can you clean bin rooms and carparks?+

Yes. We clean and deodorise bin rooms and hose bin bays where a tap and drain are available. Regular carpark sweeps are part of most schedules, and we also offer pressure washing for driveways, entries and carpark floors as a separate scoped job.

What suburbs do you cover for strata cleaning?+

Wollongong CBD and North Wollongong, Fairy Meadow, Corrimal, Thirroul, Bulli, Woonona, Figtree, Unanderra, Port Kembla, Warrawong, Dapto, Albion Park, Shellharbour, Oak Flats and Kiama.

Can you handle an urgent one-off common area clean?+

Yes. We take one-off jobs such as post-renovation corridor cleans, pre-inspection tidy-ups, graffiti-adjacent wash-downs and bin room resets, quoted per site.
