FAQ

Gift voucher questions

Does the voucher expire?

Most purchased gift vouchers must be valid for at least three years under Australian Consumer Law, unless a lawful exception applies. We confirm the expiry date and redemption terms in writing before you pay and print the expiry on the voucher. Contact us if you have an existing voucher.

Can they pick any day?

Vouchers are redeemed on weekdays (Monday to Friday), subject to team availability. The recipient simply calls or books online and mentions the voucher.

How is the voucher delivered?

Your choice: we can email the voucher to you, email it directly to the recipient, or prepare a printable voucher that our team arranges with you.

How do I pay for it?

No payment is taken on this page. Our team confirms pricing and takes payment by phone when they call to arrange the details.
