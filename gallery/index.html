Common questions

What cleaning services does this gallery show?+

The gallery features kitchens, bathrooms, ovens, carpets and living spaces, with examples of residential, end of lease and deep cleaning tasks. Use the captions to find the service that fits your property.

Do you take before and after photos of my clean?+

We can take before and after photos on request, which is especially useful for end of lease cleans where you may need evidence for your property manager or agent.

Can I request a clean that matches a specific result shown here?+

Yes, tell us which type of result you want, such as an oven detail, carpet steam clean or a full bond clean, and we will scope the job to match on your quote or booking.

Do the gallery categories match your service pages?+

Yes, categories such as End of Lease Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Carpet Cleaning and Window Cleaning link back to the matching service so you can see pricing and details before booking.

How do I book a clean like the ones in the gallery?+

You can get an instant estimate through our quote page or go straight to booking, and our team will confirm the scope based on your property and the result you want.
