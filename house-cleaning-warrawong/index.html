Cleaning priorities for Warrawong homes

  • Renters often want a tidy home before a routine inspection. Tell us the inspection date and the areas the agent usually checks, such as the oven, bathrooms and windows.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you do a clean before my rental inspection in Warrawong?+

Yes. Tell us the inspection date and the main areas the agent checks. We plan a one-off clean before that date, subject to availability. This is a regular or detailed house clean, not an end of lease clean. If you are moving out, ask about our end of lease service instead.

How much does house cleaning cost in Warrawong?+

Hourly rates start from $60/hr ex GST · $66/hr incl. GST for a weekly weekday clean (standard weekday rate $65/hr ex GST · $71.50/hr incl. GST). Fortnightly saves $2/hr ex GST · $2.20/hr incl. GST. There is a two hour minimum, so weekly cleans start from $120 ex GST · $132 incl. GST. Saturday, Sunday and NSW public holiday cleans are charged at the day rate.

Do I need to be home?+

No. Many regulars leave a key or lockbox code. All cleaners are police checked and fully insured.

Do you bring your own equipment and products?+

Yes. We bring all equipment, and eco-friendly products are available on request.

Can I skip a week or cancel?+

Yes. There are no lock-in contracts. Tell us and we will reschedule or pause your clean.

How soon can you start?+

Same-week bookings are often available. Tell us the week you need and we will confirm by call or text.
